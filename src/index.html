About CyberSafe

CyberSafe shares helpful information about online safety. Our goal is to help people understand cybersecurity and protect their personal information.

Cybersecurity Tips

Here are some important ways to stay safe online.

Strong Passwords

Use strong passwords and avoid using the same password everywhere.

Avoid Phishing

Be careful with suspicious emails and unknown links.

Protect Your Information

Do not share personal information with untrusted websites.

Keep your software updated, use two-factor authentication, and think carefully before clicking unknown links.
